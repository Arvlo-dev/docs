Projects are the foundation of every documentation site in Arvlo. Each project contains its own documentation, releases, collaborators, settings, analytics, and integrations.

This guide walks you through creating your first project and publishing your documentation.

## Before You Begin
Before creating a project, consider a few things:

- What product or service are you documenting?
- Who is your target audience?
- How would you like to organize your documentation?
- Will multiple people collaborate on the project?

Having a rough outline before you begin makes organizing your documentation much easier later.

## Creating a Project
To create a new project:

1. Sign in to Arvlo.
2. Open your dashboard.
3. Click **New Project**.
4. Enter your project information.
5. Choose the project's visibility.
6. Create the project.

Your new project will be created immediately and you'll be taken to the project dashboard.

## Project Information
Each project includes basic information that identifies your documentation.

Depending on your plan and configuration, this may include:

- Project name
- Description
- Project icon
- URL slug
- Branding
- Custom domain

Choose a clear, recognizable project name that matches your product or service.

You can update this information later from your project settings.

## Project Visibility
When creating a project, you'll choose who can access your documentation.

Public projects allow anyone with the link to view your documentation.

Private projects restrict access to authorized users and collaborators.

You can change your project's visibility later if your requirements change.

## Creating Your First Documentation Page
Once your project has been created, it's time to add your first page.

A good first page is usually:

- Welcome
- Getting Started
- Installation
- Quick Start

Create a new page, add a title and description, then begin writing your documentation using Markdown.

Every page begins as a draft, allowing you to review it before publishing.

## Publishing Your Project
Once you've written your first page, preview it to ensure everything looks correct.

When you're ready, publish the page to make it available to your users.

As your documentation grows, you can continue adding pages, organizing categories, and publishing updates whenever needed.

## What's Next?
Congratulations! You've created your first Arvlo project.

From here, you can continue building your documentation by learning how to:

- Create documentation pages.
- Organize your documentation.
- Use Markdown.
- Add custom components.
- Publish releases.
- Collaborate with your team.

Continue to **Creating Documentation** to start writing your documentation.
