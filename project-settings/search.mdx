# Search

Every published documentation site includes search. Readers can choose **Search** in the navigation or use the displayed keyboard shortcut.

Search matches the title, description, section, and rendered text of visible pages. Results respect active reader selectors, so readers do not see pages excluded by their chosen version, framework, or other context.

Queries and result counts are recorded as anonymous reader events. Searches with poor results may appear as answer gaps in **Reader insights**.
