# Receiving Suggestions

Published pages include a **Suggest a change** action. A reader enters a suggestion and may optionally add their name and email address. Arvlo associates the submission with the current page and browser session.

New suggestions appear in **Project tools → Reader insights → Suggestions** with the page, message, author details when supplied, submission time, and status.

## Enabling suggestions

Suggestions are available on published documentation by default.

This has not yet been implemented.

## Notifications

Project owners can enable Slack or Discord alerts for every new suggestion in **Project settings**. Outbound webhooks can also subscribe to the `suggestion` event.
